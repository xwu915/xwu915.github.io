PROFILE
Xiao Wu
MD  ·  Interventional Radiology  ·  UCSF

Xiao Wu is an Interventional Radiology resident at UCSF. Her research focuses on outcomes, cost-effectiveness, and evidence translation in interventional radiology and neurovascular imaging. Her work has been recognized by the Society of Interventional Radiology, the Radiological Society of North America, and JAMA Network Open. She earned her MD from Yale School of Medicine and studied Mathematics at Yale College, where she received the Elizabeth Lowell Putnam Prize in the William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competition. She will join the UCSF faculty in Interventional Radiology in September 2026.
